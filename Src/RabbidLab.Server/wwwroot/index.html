Icons and descriptions

Auto-fill descriptionsRabbid Lab recognises common gear like TrueNAS, iDRAC, ESXi and UniFi and fills in what it is. Turn it off to clear the ones it filled in. Descriptions you typed always stay.
On
Refresh icons and descriptionsReads the site of every link again, four at a time from the top: an icon from the site is brought up to date, an empty description is filled in, and a link on your network gets its IP. A picture of your own, No icon and descriptions you typed stay as they are. An internet site that turns Rabbid Lab away gets its icon from DuckDuckGo's icon service; nothing on your network is ever sent there.
