Sign-in

Session timeout and account lockout for Rabbid Lab.

Session timeout Everyone

Sign out when idleUsers are signed out after this long with no clicks, key presses or scrolling in any open tab. They are warned one minute before.

Account lockout Everyone

Lock afterEach failed sign-in adds a longer delay before the next attempt, up to 5 minutes. After this many failed attempts, the account is locked out until it is unlocked below. A locked-out administrator can still sign in with the password and an authenticator code together, or on the server itself.
Locked outAccounts that are locked out now. Unlock an account after you confirm its owner made the failed attempts.

No accounts are locked out.

Network

The ranges the network scan pings, how often it scans on its own, and how it identifies devices. Changes are saved immediately.

Ranges

Add a rangeIPv4 in CIDR form, from /20 (4,094 addresses) to /30, such as 10.10.40.0/24. Up to 16 ranges.

Schedule

Scan on its ownHow often every range that is on is scanned without anyone selecting Scan now.
Alert when a new device joinsSet up in Alerts, under Network. Devices found by the first scan are not new.
Open Alerts

Identifying devices

SNMP communityRead-only community string used to ask switches, access points and printers what they are. Windows computers are not asked. The default is public.

Each scan pings every address. Addresses that do not answer ping are tried on ports 445, 3389, 135, 22, 80 and 443, and this server's ARP table adds devices that block both. Devices that answer are checked for common open ports, their DNS and NetBIOS names, their MAC address vendor and SNMP. Nothing on a device is changed.
